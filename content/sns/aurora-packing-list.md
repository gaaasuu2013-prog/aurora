What should you wear for an aurora trip?
Here is what one of us wore in Rovaniemi, Finland, in October 2026. It felt like about −5°C at the coldest.

- Top: merino wool shirt → HEATTECH → light jacket → thick down coat
- Bottom: HEATTECH tights → warm pants
- Socks: merino five-toe socks + thick warm socks

Even so, our toes got cold during the late-night aurora watch. Next time, we plan to bring better boots.
Stick-on heat packs really helped (do not put them directly on your skin).

From December to March, official guides list about −5 to −30°C, so you need more than our October outfit.
